# Live-action research comedies

Four AI dramatizations for the existing research cards. These are conceptual comedy films, not recordings of the published experiments.

| Card | Film | Duration | Source DOI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI-powered printed electronics | Walk Naturally | 30 s | 10.1007/s10570-025-06542-7 |
| Biosensors | The Pencil Changed Jobs | 30 s | 10.1021/acsnano.4c18646 |
| Printed memory | The Boss Has Two States | 30 s | 10.1016/j.carbon.2024.119320 |
| Printed energy storage | The Ink Slept Well | 26 s | 10.1007/s42114-026-01862-z |

Each MP4 is H.264/AAC, 1280×720, 24 fps, with faststart. Matching JPG posters and English/Korean WebVTT files accompany the films. The homepage loads the MP4 only when played and displays timed captions below the picture, including in its custom fullscreen viewer.

Hanji footage was made in Google Flow. Biosensor footage combines Gemini and Pika. Memory footage was made in Pika, and ink footage in Vidu. Pika/Vidu source resolution is lower than the final edit; existing source watermarks remain. Research diagrams, source cards, and background music for the remaining three films were assembled separately. The cherry-blossom film remains in `../v3/`.

The memory story is an analogy for resistance switching and retention, based on a review article. The biosensor footage illustrates a 5hmC sensor concept, not patient diagnosis or actual apparatus. MnO₂ mitigates MXene oxidation in the ink study; the human guard and sleeping colleague are comedy, not the protection mechanism.

Claude (independent reviewer) reviewed supplied paper summaries, narrative and caption text. It did not inspect the video or audio. Final clips and edits were inspected using sampled frames and decoded in full; all four completed files were played to the end locally before integration.
