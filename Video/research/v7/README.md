# PPEL research films v7 (current homepage edition, 2026.11.10)

Ten films, two per research card, each in separate Korean and English versions with narration, background music and WebVTT captions. 1920x1080, H.264 + AAC, faststart.

Materials, devices and processes are 3D scenes rendered in Blender (Cycles). The titles, labels and number graphics of v6 are laid over them with HyperFrames. Narration is Qwen3 TTS, music is ACE Step. On screen text and numbers are the same as v6.
