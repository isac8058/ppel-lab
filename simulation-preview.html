Density functional theory

Why does this material behave this way?

  • Charge transfer at interfaces (charge density difference, Bader charge)
  • Dipole moment and polarization
  • Adsorption and binding energy of molecules such as O₂ and glucose
  • Electronic structure (DOS/PDOS) and how adsorption shifts it
  • Band structure, work function, doping, defect and surface models
